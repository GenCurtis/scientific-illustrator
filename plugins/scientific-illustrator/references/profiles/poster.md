# profile: poster

A conference poster read standing, without a narrator, typically at 1–1.5 m.

## 质量检查清单

- **90-second scan** — title and conclusions readable from a distance; the takeaway lands without reading everything.
- **Visual-first** — results dominate; text supports.
- **Discoverable reading path** — columns in a clear order; numbered steps or arrows where the flow is not obvious.
- **Complete blocks** — title/authors, introduction, methods, results, conclusions, references/acknowledgements, contact/QR.
- **Consistency** — one palette and font system; figure styles match the manuscript.
- **Print safety** — no large dark fills, no hairlines, contrast sufficient for large-format printing.

## profile_settings（宽松默认）

`canvas_mm`, `orientation`, `columns`, `min_font_pt`, `blocks`, `reading_path`, `qr_required`.

## Recreation policy interplay

Same policy semantics; `publication-ready` may fix print-safety issues (contrast, hairlines) while keeping the reference structure.

## 常见失败模式

- A template stuffed with paragraphs; body text below reading size.
- Icon salad and competing styles; no reading path.
- Too many equally sized elements; no hierarchy.
